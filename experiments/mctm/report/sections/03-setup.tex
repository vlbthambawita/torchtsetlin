\section{Experimental setup}
\label{sec:setup}

\lead{Data.} CIFAR-10, \nTrain{} training and \nTest{} test images, Booleanized with
\clsname{ColorThermometerEncoder} at \nBits{} bits per colour channel, giving Boolean tensors
of shape \inShape{}. CIFAR-10 is chosen over MNIST deliberately: a single-layer \ctm already
saturates MNIST, leaving no headroom in which a depth effect could be observed.

\lead{Architecture.} Layer~1 is a \clsname{ConvCoalescedTsetlinMachine} with \cOne{} shared
clauses and $\pOne{}\times\pOne{}$ patches at stride~1. Coalesced (shared-clause) models are
used throughout because a feature layer wants generic clauses, not clauses owned by one
class. Its spatial maps are OR-pooled $\poolK{}\times\poolK{}$ and fed to layer~2, a second
\clsname{ConvCoalescedTsetlinMachine} with \cTwo{} clauses and $\pTwo{}\times\pTwo{}$
patches, which also acts as the classifier. The effective receptive field of the stack is
$9\times9$ input pixels.

\lead{Protocol.} \epochsRun{} epochs per trainable stage, batch size \batchSize{} with the
library's default batched feedback, three seeds per arm. We report the best test accuracy
reached over the run, its spread across seeds, and wall-clock time on one RTX~3090.

\subsection{The arms}

Each arm answers one question. The design fixes the falsification criteria in advance.

\begin{center}\small
\begin{tabularx}{\textwidth}{@{}lX@{}}
\ttoprule
\thead{Arm} & \thead{Question it answers} \\
\ttmidrule
\code{single-l1}      & What layer~1 alone achieves --- the greedy ceiling it is trained against. \\
\code{single-matched} & The same \emph{total} clause budget in one layer. Does depth beat width? \\
\code{single-rf}      & One layer with patch $=$ the stack's $9\times9$ effective receptive
                        field. Is any gain just a bigger receptive field? \\
\code{flat-stack}     & Layer-1 bits, globally OR-pooled, into a flat TM. Does keeping the
                        \emph{spatial} map earn its cost over the trivial stack? \\
\code{mctm}           & The proposal. \\
\code{mctm-random}    & Random layer-1 clauses, same layer~2. Does the supervised local
                        objective buy anything? \\
\code{mctm-nopool}    & $\text{pool}=1$. Is OR-pooling load-bearing? \\
\code{mctm-nopos}     & Layer-2 position encoding off. \\
\ttbotrule
\end{tabularx}
\end{center}

\begin{ttkey}
Declared before running: the \mctm is worth pursuing only if it beats \code{single-matched}
\emph{and} \code{single-rf} \emph{and} \code{flat-stack} at matched clause budget. If
\code{mctm-random} matches \code{mctm}, the greedy supervised objective contributes nothing.
If \code{mctm} does not exceed \code{single-l1}, greedy saturation is confirmed.
\end{ttkey}
