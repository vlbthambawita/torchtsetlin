A previous report found the multilayer convolutional Tsetlin machine's \emph{architecture}
sound and its greedy supervised layer-wise \emph{training} not, to the point where random
first-layer clauses beat trained ones on CIFAR-10, and diagnosed the cause as density: a layer
trained to classify fires on \fireGreedyPre{}\% of patch positions, and a second layer over
bits that sparse can only learn negations. Its \S7.3 named three repairs. This report
implements and measures all three, at the same architecture, budget and seeds, with the
acceptance criteria fixed before any run.

\lead{The diagnosis was right and the repair is decisive.} A two-sided controller that walks
each clause towards a target firing rate --- no labels, no training, seconds of work --- lifts
the stack from \accMctm{}\% to \accMctmCalibRTwoZero{}\% over the trained first layer, and to
\sizeBestAcc{}\% once it is also told to cut each clause to \sizeBestTarget\ of its
\densityGreedySize\ literals. Run over an \emph{untrained} first layer it reaches
\randBestAcc{}\%, against \accSingleMatched{}\% and \accSingleRf{}\% for the two single-layer
baselines at the same clause budget --- the first configuration in either report for which the
second layer is worth what it costs.

\lead{An unsupervised objective wins where the architecture matters.} A patch autoencoder
(\ideaA) reaches \synNearAutoAuto{}\% on a relational task where one layer is at chance by
construction and \synXorAutoAuto{}\% where one layer is capped at $75\%$, both past the first
report's \emph{hand-built oracle}, whose exact detectors turn out to be too sparse to conjoin.
Over a repaired first layer a layer-2 clause shrinks from \synltwoNearMctmTask\ literals to
\synltwoNearCalibTask{} --- the composition the proposal predicted, and an interpretable one.
\lead{Credit propagation (\ideaC) does not survive implementation:} no forgetting term, then a
forgetting term that erases the layer, then no bandwidth, and finally drift. What both reports
now say is that the architecture and the density diagnosis were right, and the part to discard
is supervised layer-wise training itself.
