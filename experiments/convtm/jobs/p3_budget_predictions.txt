#            own headline (THEORY.md 5.5.8).                                      1.25 GPU-h
#
# A 3 x 2 factorial in {s = 2, 10, 20} x {budget none, 32}; the s = 10 column already exists, so
# only four cells are bought. `s` is the *other* dial that shortens clauses, and the admission
# threshold of THEORY.md 5.5.2 C5.1 is pi > 1/(1+s): 0.333 at s = 2, 0.091 at s = 10, 0.048 at
# s = 20.
#
# PREDICTION B1 (THEORY.md 5.5.6): L_unconstrained rises with s, therefore so does the budget
#   effect. Quantitatively: L_unc(s=2) < 32 so beta ~ 1 and Delta(s=2) vanishes into the seed
#   band; L_unc(s=20) > 147.5 and Delta(s=20) > +9.46.
# FALSIFIED BY: Delta(s=2) >= +3 pp while L_unc(s=2) <= 40; or Delta(s=20) < Delta(s=10) while
#   L_unc(s=20) > L_unc(s=10). Either kills the bloat law, which is the report's explanation of
#   why we measure +9.46 where CSC-TM measured +0.06.
#
# PREDICTION P2 (THEORY.md 5.4.4): accuracy against *achieved* firing rate collapses the budget
#   family {8,16,32,64,128,none} at s=10 (block 1) and the s family {2,10,20} unconstrained onto
#   ONE curve.
# FALSIFIED BY: at matched achieved firing rate the two families differ by more than the seed
#   band -- which would mean the cap does something beyond density control, i.e. *which*
#   literals are kept matters independently of how many.
